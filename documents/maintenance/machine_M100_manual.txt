Document ID: DOC-M100-001

Machine ID: M100

Machine Type: CNC Milling Machine

Manufacturer: ABC Industrial Systems

Operating Temperature:

The normal operating temperature of machine M100 should remain between 40°C and 65°C. Temperatures above 75°C are considered abnormal and require immediate inspection.

Cooling System:

Machine M100 uses a liquid cooling system. Coolant level and coolant flow must be inspected before every production shift.

Overheating Troubleshooting:
1. Check the coolant reservoir level.
2. Inspect coolant pipes for blockages.
3. Verify that the cooling pump is functioning.
4. Inspect the cooling fan for damage.
5. Check the temperature sensor for incorrect readings.
Preventive Maintenance:
- Cooling system inspection: Every 7 days.
- Lubrication inspection: Every 15 days.
- Complete machine inspection: Every 30 days.
Maintenance Warning:

If machine temperature exceeds 75°C, stop production and notify the maintenance team.
