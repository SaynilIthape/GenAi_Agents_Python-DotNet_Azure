Quality Inspection Report – M100

Document ID: DOC-M100-002

Machine ID: M100

Report Date: 25 October 2026

Part Number: P100

Production Batch: BATCH-2026-105

Inspection Findings:

During inspection of batch BATCH-2026-105, 18 out of 200 manufactured parts were found defective.

Observed Defects:
- Dimensional deviation beyond permitted tolerance.
- Surface finishing irregularities.
- Increased tool vibration during machining.
Investigation Findings:

The machine temperature was recorded at 82°C during production. The normal operating temperature should remain between 40°C and 65°C.

The coolant flow rate was also found to be below the recommended operating level.

Probable Root Cause:

Insufficient coolant circulation and elevated operating temperature may have contributed to dimensional deviations and inconsistent machining quality.

Corrective Actions:
1. Inspect and clean coolant pipes.
2. Verify cooling pump performance.
3. Inspect cutting tools for wear.
4. Perform dimensional inspection after corrective maintenance.
5. Resume production only after quality approval.
