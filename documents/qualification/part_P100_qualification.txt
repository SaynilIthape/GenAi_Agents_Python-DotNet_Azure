Part Qualification Record – P100

Document ID: DOC-P100-003

Part Number: P100

Part Description: Precision Aluminium Housing

Manufacturing Machine: M100

Material: Aluminium Alloy 6061

Dimensional Requirements:
- Length: 100 mm ± 0.10 mm.
- Width: 50 mm ± 0.05 mm.
- Height: 25 mm ± 0.05 mm.
Quality Requirements:
1. All dimensions must remain within specified tolerances.
2. No visible cracks or surface defects are permitted.
3. Surface finish must be Ra 1.6 µm or better.
4. All critical dimensions must be verified during first-piece inspection.
Qualification Process:
- First-piece inspection is mandatory.
- A minimum of 10 consecutive parts must pass dimensional inspection.
- Quality engineering approval is required before mass production.
Approval Status: Approved for production, subject to compliance with inspection requirements.
